## Sample and interpretation

Collection took place on 19–20 October 2026, targeting relevant 2024–2026 posts without geographic restrictions. Of 24 attempted posts, 21 yielded 264 comment or reply records. Thematic analysis retained 128 substantive top-level comments: 69 from Xiaohongshu and 59 from Instagram.

The final workbook uses report categories A–E: interest and visual appeal; product identification and sourcing; application skills and effort; service access and price information; and wear, removal and comfort. One limited-inventory comment remains under supplementary code F. Multiple coding is allowed; these counts describe comments, not consumers or market prevalence. Creator replies and service-provider offers are separated from consumer comments. Interest and enquiries do not establish purchases.
